# Proposing corrections or research

[Home](README.md)

For a correction, identify the document, exact claim, verifiable evidence, and effect on the conclusion. Preserve historical results: record a corrected conclusion in a separate dated entry linking to the earlier one.

For a new experiment, specify the model, data, unknowns, success criterion, synthetic controls, resource limits, and resume procedure in advance. Explain how it differs from completed series. Distinguish timeouts, zero candidates in a partial range, and exact contradictions.

New published results should include versions, hashes, raw counts, and clear limits of reproducibility. For statistical work, distinguish reuse of the archive from independent validation. Do not add connection details, personal wallets, or infrastructure secrets to research materials.

For translation corrections, identify the Russian passage and its English counterpart; do not change numerical results or silently strengthen a conclusion. Update both language indexes when adding a report.

The license for future contributions must be explicitly agreed at publication; this snapshot does not choose one automatically. [Provenance and rights](NOTICE.md).
